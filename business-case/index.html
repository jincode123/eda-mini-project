In 2024, the number of incidents increased by approximately 11% compared to 2023. This upward trend raises concerns about operational risk and highlights the need to better understand the underlying factors contributing to these incidents. The goal of this exploratory data analysis (EDA) is to investigate potential root causes of the increase in incidents by examining trip characteristics, route-level patterns, and compensation structures. Through data-driven exploration, this analysis aims to identify whether certain trips or routes carry higher risk and whether these risks are appropriately reflected in operational decisions. Specifically, this analysis seeks to answer the following questions:

  • - Are longer trips riskier than shorter trips?

  • - Are certain routes riskier than others?

  • - Is the company adequately compensated for higher-risk trips?

  • - Which routes have the highest incident rate per trip (or per mile)?

  • - Where do incidents occur most frequently?

  • - Are some routes statistically more dangerous than others?

By addressing these questions, the analysis provides insights that can support safety improvements, operational planning, and further statistical investigation.

safety_df['incident_date']=pd.to_datetime(safety_df['incident_date'])
safety_df['year']=pd.to_datetime(safety_df['incident_date']).dt.year

yearly_incidents = (
    safety_df.groupby('year')['incident_id']

)
